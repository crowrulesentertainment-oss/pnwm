Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

In plain language: PNWM website forms may collect contact details and the information you choose to send so that the community initiative can respond. PNWM does not request or accept money, payments, financial pledges, or monetary donations through this website.

1. Who operates this website

Pacific Northwest Mother's (“PNWM”) is a community initiative operated by CrowRules Entertainment. It is not a government agency and is not represented as a registered nonprofit or charity. Privacy questions can be sent to crowrulesentertainment@gmail.com.

2. Information you may submit

Depending on the form you choose, you may provide your name, email address, city or general area, availability, volunteer interests, event or meeting interest, resource suggestions, project ideas, partnership inquiries, or a message requesting community information or support. Please provide only what is necessary for your request.

Do not submit Social Security numbers, payment or bank information, passwords, account credentials, medical records, diagnoses, immigration or custody details, children's identifying information, exact home addresses, or detailed descriptions of trauma or family crises. The site is not designed to collect sensitive information or emergency reports.

3. Why information is used

Information submitted through a form may be used to review your submission, respond to your inquiry, communicate about the activity you selected, assess a resource suggestion, coordinate volunteer interest, prevent abuse, and maintain basic records of the initiative. A submission is not a guarantee of acceptance, services, supplies, a volunteer role, or a partnership.

4. Website hosting and service providers

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The site uses Supabase services to store and retrieve some resource listings, event/program information, and form submissions. The page also loads third-party website assets such as fonts and a JavaScript library from external content-delivery services. Those providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and request logs under their own policies. External event, resource, or service links take you to third-party sites whose privacy practices PNWM does not control.

5. Access, sharing, and publication

PNWM does not sell submitted personal information. Form submissions are intended for authorized review and response, not public display. Publicly published resources, event information, project updates, and aggregate counts may appear on the website. Do not include personal information about another person in a suggestion or message unless you have permission and it is necessary.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect the safety of people, investigate abuse, or protect the website and initiative. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Retention and requests

Information should be kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, administer the relevant activity, address safety or operational issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by form and record type. To ask what information you submitted or request correction or deletion, email crowrulesentertainment@gmail.com and identify the form or submission as best you can. PNWM may need to verify a request and may retain information where a legal or legitimate operational reason applies.

7. Children and family information

This website is intended for adults participating in community activities. A parent or caregiver should not submit a child's identifying or sensitive information through PNWM forms. PNWM does not intentionally seek to collect personal information directly from children.

8. Cookies and analytics

PNWM does not intentionally use this site to run targeted advertising or sell personal data. Hosting, external assets, or service providers may use technical logs or technologies necessary to deliver and secure their services. The current site should not be assumed to have no third-party processing simply because PNWM does not operate an advertising program.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, forms, providers, or practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised when material changes are made. Please review this page periodically.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: crowrulesentertainment@gmail.com.

This page describes intended website practices and is not a guarantee of legal compliance. The operator should verify it against actual database access, retention, and incident-response practices before relying on it as a final legal policy.
