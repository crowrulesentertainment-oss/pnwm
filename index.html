What it is

A community built around mothers.

Pacific Northwest Mother's exists to create a place where mothers can find information, meet other mothers, discover opportunities, locate existing services, and eventually receive direct assistance when life becomes overwhelming.

Why Pacific Northwest Mother's exists

Motherhood can be beautiful, exhausting, rewarding, isolating, expensive and overwhelming — sometimes all at the same time.

This initiative is about building a community around the people who spend so much of their lives caring for everyone else.

The goal is simple: help mothers find help, find community, and know they are not forgotten.

What it's for

From connection to action.

The long-term vision is a community where people can participate at whatever level they are comfortable with.

01 — CONNECT

Find community

Meet other mothers through online spaces, conversations, gatherings and future local meetings.

02 — DISCOVER

Find resources

Explore existing programs for food, housing, healthcare, childcare, parenting and family support.

03 — PARTICIPATE

Get involved

Volunteer, share skills, organize events, sponsor programs, or simply spread the word.

04 — GIVE BACK

Help another mother

As the program grows, community-generated revenue can help fund direct assistance and future programs.
