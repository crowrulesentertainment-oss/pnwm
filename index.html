What it is

A community built around mothers.

Pacific Northwest Mother's exists to create a place where mothers can find information, meet other mothers, discover opportunities, locate existing services, and eventually receive direct assistance when life becomes overwhelming.

Why Pacific Northwest Mother's exists

Motherhood can be beautiful, exhausting, rewarding, isolating, expensive and overwhelming — sometimes all at the same time.

This initiative is about building a community around the people who spend so much of their lives caring for everyone else.

The goal is simple: help mothers find help, find community, and know they are not forgotten.

PNWM give-back projects

Small acts. Real support. Stronger families.

Our first community initiative is Mother & Baby Essentials — a volunteer-led supply drive connecting approved baby and family essentials with established local organizations across the South Sound and Western Washington.

Community participation is non-monetary only.

PNWM does not request or accept money, payments, financial pledges, or fundraising contributions. This project may coordinate volunteer interest and physical items only after a receiving organization confirms its current needs and intake arrangements. PNWM is not represented as a registered charity, and no tax benefit is promised.

Mother & Baby Essentials

Help gather items that partner organizations have confirmed they can accept. Priorities may include new diapers, wipes, unopened hygiene supplies, and clean baby clothing in requested sizes.

  • Suggest a local organization that could receive supplies.

About this community initiative

Pacific Northwest Mother's (PNWM) is a community initiative operated by CrowRules Entertainment. It is not a government agency and is not presented as a registered nonprofit or charity. PNWM does not request or accept money, payments, financial pledges, or monetary donations. Volunteer participation and any in-kind item coordination are subject to review and confirmed arrangements.

Resource and event information may change; please confirm details directly with the organization providing the service. Do not submit urgent requests, medical details, children's personal information, or other sensitive information through website forms.
