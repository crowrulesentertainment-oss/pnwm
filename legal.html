Legal Notice & Website Terms

Effective date: October 12, 2026

No monetary solicitation. PNWM does not request or accept money, payments, monetary donations, financial pledges, crowdfunding, or fundraising contributions through this website. No payment or tax-deductibility claim is made. Any in-kind item activity is separate, voluntary, and must not be represented as a cash contribution.

1. Operator and status

Pacific Northwest Mother's (“PNWM”) is a community initiative operated by CrowRules Entertainment. PNWM is not a government agency and is not represented by this website as a registered nonprofit, tax-exempt organization, or registered charity. The website is intended to share community information, resource listings, event/program information, and ways to express interest in volunteering or suggesting projects.

2. Information, not a promise of services

PNWM is a community information and coordination initiative, not a hospital, healthcare provider, legal service, government benefits office, crisis service, or emergency response organization. A resource listing is provided for convenience and does not mean PNWM endorses, operates, or guarantees that service. Program eligibility, availability, fees, hours, contact details, and requirements can change. Confirm information directly with the organization responsible for the service.

Nothing on the website is medical, legal, financial, or professional advice. If you face immediate danger or a medical emergency, contact local emergency services. Do not use website forms for urgent requests.

3. Forms and submissions

Submitting a form does not guarantee a response, assistance, acceptance into a program, a volunteer role, a partnership, or delivery of supplies. Submissions may be reviewed by an authorized coordinator and may be declined. Please submit accurate information and only details necessary to explain your inquiry.

Do not submit passwords, payment details, Social Security numbers, sensitive medical information, details identifying children, or private information about another person. PNWM may reject, remove, or decline to act on spam, unlawful material, threats, abusive messages, or information that creates a safety or privacy concern.

4. Volunteers and in-kind items

Volunteer-interest forms are not job offers, confirmed shifts, or guarantees of acceptance. Before participating, volunteers should receive clear instructions about their role, supervision, safety, transportation, privacy, and any screening requirements. Do not enter private homes, handle confidential family records, or interact with children outside an approved and appropriately supervised arrangement.

Do not bring or ship items to PNWM or any listed organization unless a current needs list, receiving organization, and intake/drop-off arrangement have been confirmed. Potential organizations mentioned on the site are not PNWM partners unless an authorized, current written agreement exists and permission to identify the organization publicly has been obtained. PNWM does not promise that an item will reach a particular person or family. Item counts, if shown, should reflect verified records and may be corrected.

5. External links and third-party services

The website may link to external organizations, registration pages, event listings, maps, or resources. Those third parties operate independently and have their own terms, privacy policies, and service availability. PNWM is not responsible for third-party content or services and does not claim affiliation without explicit confirmation.

6. Website content and permitted use

Unless otherwise noted, original PNWM website text and design are maintained by the initiative/operator. Names, logos, photographs, and materials belonging to other organizations remain with their respective owners. Do not reuse another party's material in a way that implies endorsement or violates its rights. If you submit content, submit only material you have the right to share and avoid private or identifying information about others.

7. Availability, accuracy, and limitation

The website and its listings are provided for general community information on an “as available” basis. PNWM makes reasonable efforts to review information but cannot guarantee that every listing is current, complete, accessible, or suitable for a particular person's needs. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the operator disclaims implied warranties and is not responsible for third-party actions, inaccurate external listings, or interruptions beyond its reasonable control. Nothing in this notice excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

8. Respectful and lawful use

Do not misuse forms, attempt unauthorized access, interfere with website operation, submit fraudulent information, harass participants, or publish another person's personal information without a lawful basis. PNWM may restrict or report misuse where appropriate.

9. Changes and contact

These terms may be updated as the initiative and website change. Material updates will be reflected in the effective date above. Contact crowrulesentertainment@gmail.com with questions about this notice.

This notice is a practical website disclosure, not legal advice. The operator should have Washington counsel review it against the actual organization structure, programs, and practices.
